# 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

*[English below](#code-of-conduct-english)*

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1 行为准则。

## 我们的承诺

为营造开放、友好的社区,我们承诺让每一位参与者都免于骚扰,无论其年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

## 行为标准

**鼓励的行为**:体谅他人、尊重不同意见与经历、礼貌地给予并接受建设性反馈、为错误负责并致歉、以社区整体利益为重。

**不可接受的行为**:性化言语 / 行为或性骚扰;辱骂、侮辱、贬损性评论与人身 / 政治攻击;公开或私下骚扰;未经许可公开他人隐私信息;其他在专业场合不当的行为。

## 执行

可将滥用、骚扰或其他不当行为通过 **GitHub 私有渠道**(仓库的 Security Advisory 或 Issue,涉敏感信息请私下联系维护者)举报。维护者有权且有责任移除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 等,并就管理决定酌情说明理由。

完整条款(含执行细则与影响分级)见 Contributor Covenant v2.1 原文。

## Code of Conduct (English)

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

### Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Standards

**Encouraged**: empathy and kindness; respect for differing opinions and experiences; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes; focusing on what's best for the community.

**Unacceptable**: sexualized language/imagery or unwelcome sexual attention; trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without permission; other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers (via the repo's Security Advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly for sensitive matters). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Full terms (including enforcement guidelines and impact tiers) are in the Contributor Covenant v2.1 text linked above.
